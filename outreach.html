Before you apply

Curiosity is welcome.
Commitment is essential.

You don’t need robotics experience to join. We’re glad to help you learn—but every member should be ready to contribute consistently.

  • 01
    Show up consistently

    Be available for team meetings on weekdays and weekends.

  • 02
    Bring real effort

    Take ownership, collaborate, and follow through on your work.

  • 03
    Learn with the team

    Beginners are welcome. A willingness to learn matters most.
