Join Surf

Become Part of the Team

Interested in robotics, engineering, programming, design, or simply learning something new? You do not need previous robotics experience to join Surf Robotics.

We are happy to help new members learn. What matters most is commitment, effort, teamwork, and a willingness to improve.

Team members should be able to participate in meetings on weekdays and weekends, especially during the FTC competition season.
